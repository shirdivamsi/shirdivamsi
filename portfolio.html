AI & Machine Learning Portfolio

A collection of coursework, projects, research, and professional reflections completed throughout my graduate studies at Indiana Wesleyan University.

Portfolio Overview

This portfolio documents my learning journey in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. The six featured artifacts demonstrate my professional growth, understanding of AI history and foundations, ability to analyze modern Machine Learning and Deep Learning techniques, applied reasoning through AI-guided coaching conversations, and critical thinking around AI ethics and accountability. Together, they reflect my development as a data professional with a growing foundation in Artificial Intelligence.

As I continue my graduate studies, this portfolio will expand with additional AI projects, research assignments, laboratory activities, and practical implementations that demonstrate my continued growth in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Analytics.

Feedback & Continuous Improvement

My instructor reviewed this portfolio and highlighted Artifact 5 for going beyond definitions into applied, data-driven decision-making, and noted that the professional reflections effectively show how feedback shaped my reasoning. The one growth area identified was to strengthen future artifacts with measurable results and technical demonstrations, such as dataset information, performance metrics, visualizations, or code links, rather than conceptual discussion alone.

How I integrated this feedback: I added a new Technical Illustration section to Artifact 5, applying each scenario's reasoning to a small sample dataset, including an accuracy calculation for the supervised learning example, a comparison table for the clustering example, and a visualization of reward improvement over repeated attempts for the reinforcement learning example. Going forward, I plan to carry this same standard into future artifacts, pairing conceptual reasoning with concrete, measurable evidence wherever the artifact allows for it.
